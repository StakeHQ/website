The problem

Good income should mean good credit. Often it does not.

Most consumer credit still depends on collateral, a thick credit file, or both. Millions of people with steady, well-paid work have neither in a form lenders can verify, so they are declined or priced as if they were high risk. The income is real. Lenders just cannot see it or collect against it. We think that should be fixed.

What we do

Underwrite on income. Collect at the source.

01

Verified income

Lenders see verified employment and income from the borrower's actual source of pay, not a self-reported form.

02

Repayment at the source

With the borrower's consent, repayments are collected automatically when income is paid, before it reaches their account. Lenders are first in line, every pay cycle.

03

No collateral required

Because income is verified and repayment is pre-authorized, lenders can extend unsecured credit to borrowers they could not serve before, at terms that reflect the real risk.

Who it is for

Built for everyone in the chain.

For lenders

Reach borrowers with verified income and pre-authorized repayment at the source of funds. You keep your underwriting, your capital, and your customer relationship. Stake supplies the verification and repayment rails.

For employers and payment platforms

Give the people you pay access to fair credit, with no balance-sheet exposure and no change to how you run payroll. Stake handles verification, consent, and repayment collection.

For borrowers

Credit on fair terms, based on the work you already do. You decide what a lender can see, and repayments come out of your pay automatically so you never miss one.

Why it matters

This is someone's income and someone's credit.

How we build matters as much as what we build. These are the commitments the rest of the system is designed around.

What we hold to

Consent first

Nothing about a borrower is shared, and no repayment is collected, without their explicit authorization. They can see it and revoke it.

Compliance by design

Lenders lend under their own licenses and laws. Stake is built so every party stays inside its own regulatory lane.

Correct before fast

This is people's income and people's credit. We build for correctness first and speed second.

Credit built on income.

Stake connects lenders with borrowers whose income is verified at the source, and collects repayment from that income before it reaches the borrower. Better borrowers for lenders. Better rates for borrowers. A free benefit for employers.

The problem

Good income should mean good credit. Often it does not.

Most consumer credit still depends on collateral, a thick credit file, or both. Many people with steady, well-paid work have neither in a form lenders can verify, so they are declined or priced as if they were high risk. Lenders, meanwhile, compete for the same small pool of borrowers they can already see. The income is real. Nobody can see it or collect against it. We fix that.

What we do

Underwrite on income. Collect at the source.

01

Verified income

Lenders see employment and income confirmed at the borrower's actual source of pay, not a self-reported form.

02

Repayment from pay

With the borrower's consent, repayments are collected automatically when income is paid, before the money can be spent. Nothing to remember, nothing to miss.

03

Better terms for real risk

Because income is verified and repayment is pre-authorized, lenders can serve borrowers they could not reach before, at rates that reflect the real risk rather than the missing paperwork.

Who it is for

Built for lenders, employers, and the people they pay.

For lenders

Better borrowers, lower risk.

Originate to borrowers with verified income and repayment pre-authorized from their pay, introduced through the employers and payroll platforms we work with, or bring your own customers and use the rails alone. You keep your underwriting, your capital, and your customer relationship.

For employers and payroll platforms

A benefit that costs you nothing.

Give the people you pay access to credit on better terms than they can get on their own. No fees, no capital at risk, no change to how you run payroll, and nothing to administer. Stake handles verification, consent, and repayment collection.

For borrowers

Better rates, repaid from your pay.

Credit priced on the work you already do, not on collateral you do not have. You decide what a lender can see, and repayments come out of your pay automatically so you never miss one.

Correct before fast

This is someone's income and someone's credit. We build for correctness first and speed second.

What we build

The system behind credit
built on income.

We build the platform that connects lenders, employers, and borrowers, verifies income at the source, and collects repayment from pay. Fewer declined borrowers, fewer missed payments, and terms that reflect real risk.

How it works.

01

Connect

An employer or payroll platform connects to Stake. Nothing changes about how they pay people.

02

Apply and consent

A borrower applies and authorizes exactly what a lender may see and what may be collected from their pay.

03

Underwrite on income

The lender sees verified employment and income and makes its own credit decision, under its own license.

04

Repay from pay

Repayments are collected automatically when income is paid, every pay cycle, until the loan is done.

Verification

Income you can trust

Employment and income confirmed at the source of pay, so lenders underwrite on facts rather than self-reported figures.

Consent

The borrower stays in control

Borrowers authorize exactly what a lender can see and what can be collected. Authorization is explicit, visible, and revocable.

Repayment

Collected at the source

Repayments are taken automatically when income is paid, before it reaches the borrower's account. Lenders are first in line every pay cycle.

Compliance

Built into the rails

Lenders lend under their own licenses and jurisdiction. Stake is designed so that every party, lender, employer, platform, and borrower, stays within its own regulatory boundary.

Integration

Fits how you already work

Lenders and payroll platforms connect through a simple interface or API. No change to how employers run payroll, and no new capital or credit exposure for them.

Distribution

Borrowers who are ready

Through the employers and payroll platforms we work with, lenders meet applicants who already have verified income and pre-authorized repayment. Lenders can also bring their own customers and use the rails alone.

The bar we hold

Correct first, fast second.

This is people's income and people's credit history. We prioritize getting it right, build in the checks that prevent mistakes, and validate before anything reaches production. Speed follows from getting the foundations right.

The company

A small team with
a long horizon.

Stake is a focused group of engineers and operators building the infrastructure beneath modern financial markets. We work deliberately, hold a high bar, and let the results speak before we do.

Why we exist

The systems that markets depend on should be worthy of that trust.

Financial markets run on infrastructure most people never see — the layer that has to be correct every time, under every condition. Too much of it was built for a different era. We are here to build it properly: secure, resilient, and honest about the weight it carries.

We are early, and intentionally so. Rather than announce, we would rather earn. When there is something meaningful to share, we will.

What guides us

Principles we do not trade away.

Correctness

Right beats fast

In this domain, a near-miss is a miss. We would rather move carefully and be trusted than move loudly and be forgotten.

Ownership

We hold the whole problem

No handoffs at the hard part. The people who design a system are accountable for how it behaves in the real world, at 3 a.m., on the worst day.

Clarity

Simple is a feature

Complexity is a liability we pay for forever. We invest heavily in keeping systems — and explanations — as simple as the problem allows.

Discretion

Quiet by default

We say what we can stand behind and no more. Confidence, for us, sounds like restraint.
